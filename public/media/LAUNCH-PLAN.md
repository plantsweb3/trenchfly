1. Apply `identity/avatar.png` and `social/x-header.png` to @RobinFlyPons. Use the profile bio and website field in `copy/X-POSTS.md`.

4. Use the pinned six-post thread in `copy/X-POSTS.md`, with the landscape campaign art on its first post. Pin the first post after publishing.

Start/stop controls and local operations notes are supplied separately to the owner. No server purchase or paid provider upgrade is part of this launch package.
